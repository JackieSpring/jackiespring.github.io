Homework 3

Aggregate penetration score of n systems under m attackers. Each attacker meets n systems in sequence and collects an i.i.d. score at every step. The page simulates m independent attackers for two score models and compares the total, the average and the normalized total as n grows, in light of the law of large numbers and the central limit theorem. Everything runs in your browser.

SetupModel

Attacker j produces increments X1, X2, … i.i.d. with mean μ and variance σ². After n systems its total score is Sn. The simulation fills an m × n matrix of increments and uses the row sums as m independent copies of Sn, for n up to 10,000 (checkpoints from 1 to 10,000, 24 in all).

Convergence in probabilityLaw of large numbers

Fraction of attackers whose average score is farther than ε from μ, against n. Chebyshev gives P(|Sn/n − μ| > ε) ≤ σ²/(nε²), which tends to 0, so Sn/n → μ in probability.

Answering the assignmentReading the results for n systems and m attackers

Method. Increments come from a seeded 128-bit generator (sfc32); Rademacher draws use one random bit per step. Each attacker is simulated for 10,000 systems and its running total is stored at 24 checkpoints. Kolmogorov distance is sup|Fm − Φ| of the standardized totals (Sn − nμ)/(σ√n). The Berry–Esseen bound is 0.4748·E|X − μ|³/(σ³√n).
